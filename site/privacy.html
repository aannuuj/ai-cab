Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. AI-Cab has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your progress (saved words, favourites, streaks, quiz results, settings) is stored only on your device, in a container shared with the AI-Cab widgets.

Network requests

  • Content updates: the app downloads new word packs as static files. These requests contain no personal data.
  • "Draft with AI" (optional, Pro): when you tap it, the word you typed is sent to our server, which asks an AI model to draft a definition. We don't store the request.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We don't run a push server.

Photos

If you tap "Save to Photos", the app asks for add-only access to save the word card image you created. It never reads your library.
